Privacy policy

Pi Gmail Attachments is a personal, single-user tool. It is used only by the owner of the Google account that authorises it.

  • Access: read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete or change email.
  • What it does: when the owner asks, it downloads attachments from specific messages to the owner's private home server.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared, or sent to any third party. Nothing is used for advertising.
  • Storage: downloaded files and the access token stay on the owner's own devices.
  • Revoking: the owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
